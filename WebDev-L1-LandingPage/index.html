Our Craft

Painted by hand, stitched to last

Truck art began as a way for drivers to mark their vehicles as their own — every panel painted by hand, every route retold in colour. Rangreza pays the same artists to paint canvas panels instead of steel, then works with textile units in Karachi to turn those panels into pieces built for daily use.

Nothing here is printed from a photograph. Each motif is repainted for fabric, colour-matched by the same hand that painted the original, so no two runs are ever identical.
